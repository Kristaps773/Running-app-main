OrienteerRun (“the App”) is a free Android training app that generates GPS-verified orienteering-style running routes. This policy explains what data is involved when you use the App.

Summary

  • No user accounts, ads, or in-app purchases.
  • Location is used to generate routes and track runs.
  • Route and run history stay on your device.
  • Optional crash diagnostics are sent to Sentry.
  • Map and routing use public OpenStreetMap-related services.

Location

While you generate a route or run with tracking active, the App uses precise location (GPS) via a foreground service and a visible notification. Location is used only for placing checkpoints, verifying visits, showing your position, and computing distance/pace. We do not request background location when you are not tracking a run. Location is not sold.

Local storage

Routes, run history, and preferences are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to an OrienteerRun backend (the App has no user accounts).

Crash diagnostics (Sentry)

If configured, the App may send crash reports and limited performance data to Sentry (device model, OS, app version, stack traces). This helps fix bugs. Data is not sold or used for ads. See Sentry’s privacy policy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can deny location or notification permission in Android settings, clear app data, or uninstall. Contact us with privacy questions using the email above.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
