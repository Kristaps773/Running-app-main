Privacy Policy — OrienteerRun

Last updated: 25 October 2026
Contact: kristapsduda773@gmail.com

OrienteerRun (“the App”) is a free Android training app that generates GPS-verified orienteering-style running routes. This policy explains what data is involved when you use the App.

Summary

  • No user accounts, ads, or in-app purchases.
  • Location is used to generate routes and track runs.
  • Route and run history stay on your device unless you export or share them.
  • Optional crash and performance diagnostics may be sent to Sentry.
  • Maps and routing use public OpenStreetMap-related services; tile requests reveal approximate map area to the selected provider.

Location

While you generate a route or run with tracking active, the App uses precise location (GPS), and may also use approximate location. Tracking runs through a foreground service with a visible notification (notification permission on Android 13+). Location is used only for placing checkpoints, verifying visits, showing your position, and computing distance/pace. We do not request the Android “background location” permission for continuous tracking when you are not in an active run session. Location is not sold and is not shared with other users.

Local storage

Routes, run history, map preferences, and a local map-tile cache are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to an OrienteerRun backend (the App has no user accounts). Android backup, if enabled on your device, may include app data according to your system settings.

Exports you choose to share

If you use GPX or PDF export, the App writes a file on your device and opens the system share sheet. You choose the destination. Exported files can include checkpoint coordinates, planned path geometry, and—for run exports—recorded GPS track points and timestamps. OrienteerRun does not upload those files to its own servers.

Crash diagnostics (Sentry)

If configured, the App may send crash reports and limited performance data to Sentry (device model, OS, app version, stack traces, sampled performance traces). Default personally identifiable information is not attached by the App. Data is not sold or used for ads. See Sentry’s privacy policy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it if you are under the age required in your country for consent to such processing.

Your choices

You can deny location or notification permission, change map style, clear app data or uninstall, and skip GPX/PDF share if you do not want files leaving the device. Contact us with privacy questions using the email above.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date will change accordingly. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
