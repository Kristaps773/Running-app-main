## Full description
OrienteerRun is a free Android training app for orienteering-style running. Set a target distance, generate a loop of GPS checkpoints on real walking paths, then run the course with live map guidance and proximity verification.

Features:
• Random loop routes for your chosen distance
• Checkpoints snapped to walkable paths (OSRM / OpenStreetMap)
• Live map, distance, and checkpoint progress while you run
• Offline-friendly history of past routes and runs on your device
• No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Routing and map services use public OSM-related APIs.
