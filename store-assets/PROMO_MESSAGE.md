## Short post (friends / WhatsApp / Telegram / Discord / X)

I built **OrienteerRun**, a free Android app that turns a normal run into a mini orienteering course.

You pick a distance, it generates a **GPS loop with real checkpoints** on walkable paths near you, then you run it with a live map. No accounts, no ads, no subscriptions.

I made it because most running apps only track what you already did. I wanted something that **gives you a course outdoors**: structure and variety without a printed map or a gym treadmill.

It is in **closed beta**. Join as a tester (same Google account as on your phone):

## Longer post (Facebook / LinkedIn / blog / email)

**OrienteerRun: GPS orienteering style runs (closed Android beta)**

Hi, I’m Kristaps. I built OrienteerRun for runners who are tired of the same out and back and don’t need another step counter.

**What it is**  
A free Android training app: set a target distance, generate a loop of real world GPS checkpoints snapped to walking paths (OpenStreetMap), then run the course with live map guidance and checkpoint progress.

**Why I built it**  
I wanted outdoor runs that feel more like **orienteering**: a clear course, checkpoints to hit, a reason to explore streets and parks, without needing a club event or a paper map every time. Existing apps were great at logging miles; few actually *create* a course for you.

**What you get**
1. Random loop routes for the distance you choose  
2. Checkpoints on walkable paths  
3. Live map, distance, and checkpoint progress while you run  
4. Run history saved on your device  
5. No accounts, no ads, no in app purchases  

**Join the closed beta** (same Google account as on your phone):

1. Join the tester group: https://groups.google.com/g/orienteerrun-testers  
2. Opt in to closed testing: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.orienteer.app  
3. Install from Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.orienteer.app  

I’d love feedback on whether route generation works in your neighborhood and whether the checkpoint flow feels clear on a real run.
